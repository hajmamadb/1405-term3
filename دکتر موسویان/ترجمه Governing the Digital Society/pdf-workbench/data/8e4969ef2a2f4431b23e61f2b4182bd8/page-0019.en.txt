Michelle O’ Sullivan is a Lecturer in Industrial Relations in the Kemmy Business School at
the University of Limerick. She teaches in the areas of dispute resolution, negotiation
skills and employment relations to undergraduate and postgraduate students. Her primary
area of research expertise is on precarious work with a focus on public policy in regulating
precarious work. Her widely published research has investigated zero hours work, low
wage service sector jobs and wage setting in low paid jobs. Her other research interests
include trade unions, collective action, employee voice and silence, migrant workers,
dispute resolution, workplace bullying and employment law.
