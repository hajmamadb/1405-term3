Technical elements

While the academic literature on DOSNs highlights various technical aspects
of decentralization (Guidi et al. 2019; La Cava et al. 2021), we limit our focus to
three recurring technical elements: the choice for (1) open-source software,
(2) software protocols, and (3) data servers. These prime technical choices
are often premeditated by value negotiations regarding the degree of open-
ness a platform should aspire to, weighing arguments for openness against
those for “closedness.” In this context, “openness” can imply accessibility,
inclusiveness, or modifiability, while “closedness” encompasses values such
