of the aims to a flag is not an excessive burden but does create transparency
and accountability on the legitimacy of flags by state bodies. Finally, a flag by
a state body should include an account of why the right of the internet user
is outweighed by societies’ needs, as well as an indication why the sought
remedy is the appropriate way to fulfill those needs. Although this is not a
scalable solution, it is possible to achieve this in the case-by-case context
of trusted flagging. These are simple solutions to ensure that a valuable
addition to the content moderation process—state bodies functioning as
trusted flaggers—gains legitimacy and is ensured to respect the right to
freedom of expression of internet users.
