TA: You mentioned the opacity of the platform ecosystem, which brings us
to another aspect of governance, namely the rules and policies that regulate
users on platforms. As you began through the Twitter case study (Goanta
2023a) and now moving into research on TikTok platform documentation, what
does examining the perspective of the platform reveal about the regulation
of monetization?
