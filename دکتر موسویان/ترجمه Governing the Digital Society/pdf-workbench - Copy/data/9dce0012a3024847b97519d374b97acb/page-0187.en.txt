      As of September 2023, the mechanisms ErnieBot uses to circumvent
       inappropriate conversations can be readily bypassed. The system can be
       manipulated with subtle adjustments to the input, embedding it within a
        scenario. For example, rather than directly soliciting the bot’s criticism on
       governmental policies, one can frame a prompt asking ErnieBot to assist
        in understanding a friend who holds critical views on the issue. Examples
       are presented in table 10.1.
