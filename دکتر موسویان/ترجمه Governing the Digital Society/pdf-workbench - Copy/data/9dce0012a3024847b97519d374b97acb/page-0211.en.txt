About the Authors

Michiel de Lange is assistant professor in new media studies at Utrecht
University and cofounder of the Urban Interfaces research group.

Erna Ruijer is assistant professor at the Utrecht University School of
Governance.

Krisztina Varró is assistant professor in human geography and spatial
planning at Utrecht University.
